Open the game ↗

Run it on your server

Use Docker Compose and choose how long to keep your games. Optional player profiles keep stats and friends across tables. You can also enable Revoked sign-in and share your current table through your Revoked vault.

mkdir -p showdown && cd showdown
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/argseby/Showdown/main/docker-compose.yml
docker compose up -d

Open http://localhost:8080 after startup. Use HTTPS for voice and video on a public domain.
